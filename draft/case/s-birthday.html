壽星日：台灣生日優惠整理站Birthday Deals

為什麼做Why

生日優惠散在各品牌公告、部落格懶人包跟社群貼文裡，而且過期得很快。要用的那一天才去找，通常來不及。Birthday offers are scattered across brand announcements, blog round-ups and social posts, and they go stale fast. Searching on the day itself is usually too late.

使用者是誰Who it is for

一年只會用一次的人。這決定了整個產品：留存不能看次日留存，要看「隔年還會不會回來」；提醒要提前，因為一半的優惠當天辦來不及。People who use it once a year. That shapes everything: retention is not day-1 but next-year, and reminders must be early, because half the offers cannot be claimed same-day.

規模Scale

581 筆優惠、22 個縣市、4,700 家對得上的門市；154 個版本、92 支回歸測試；企劃、前後端、資料整理與分析都一個人做。581 offers, 22 counties, 4,700 matched stores; 154 releases, 92 regression tests; product, front and back end, data and analysis all solo.

怎麼分析Analysis

把資料丟進 SQLite 問十一個問題：哪一類最大方、多少優惠其實當天辦來不及、住不同縣市差多少、資料本身有多可信。供給面算完才發現需求跟供給是錯開的——零售美妝只佔 3.3% 的優惠數，卻吃掉 9.3% 的收藏。Eleven questions in SQL: which category is most generous, how many offers cannot be claimed same-day, how much location matters, how trustworthy the data is. The supply-demand gap only showed up once both sides were measured: cosmetics is 3.3% of supply but 9.3% of saves.

做了什麼決定Decisions

過期自動下架（靠人記得一定會漏）、來源分級顯示在卡片上、提前提醒、下一批優先談美妝與飲料。都是從上面那些數字推出來的。Auto-expiry by valid_until, source tier shown on each card, early reminders, and cosmetics and drinks first in the next sourcing round. Each one follows from a number above.

結果Outcome

11,205 次收藏、4,784 次回報、98% 兌換成功率。全部跑在免費方案上，一次流量超額之後把每日用量砍掉 25%。11,205 saves, 4,784 usage reports, 98% redemption success, all on free tiers, with daily usage cut 25% after one quota overrun.

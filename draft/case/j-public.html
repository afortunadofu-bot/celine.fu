背景Context

桃園市政府的一份材料設備送審文件，在我接手前已經來回修改四個月。A Taoyuan City Government material-and-equipment submission that had been going back and forth for four months before I took it over.

問題Problem

每退一次就改一次，改完再送、再退。沒有人去問「到底是哪一條不過」。Each rejection produced an edit, another submission, another rejection. Nobody had asked which specific clause was failing.

怎麼分析Analysis

把退件意見逐條對回規範條文，發現問題不在規格本身，而在比較表的呈現方式——審查委員沒辦法一眼對上規範與型錄。那是文件結構的問題，不是產品的問題。Mapping every comment back to its spec clause showed the issue was not the specification but the comparison table: the reviewer could not line up the spec against the catalogue at a glance. A document-structure problem, not a product problem.

解法Solution

重建規範比較文件，讓每一條規範、投標型錄、送審型號三欄並排對齊；同時重新打通與審查單位的溝通，當面確認理解一致再送。Rebuilt the comparison package so spec clause, tendered catalogue and submitted model sat in three aligned columns; reopened the line to the reviewing committee to confirm understanding before resubmitting.

結果Outcome

一個月內核准。後續也由我撰寫標案建議書，取得台電與桃園市政府的標案。Cleared in one month. I went on to author the RFP responses that won Taipower and Taoyuan City Government awards.

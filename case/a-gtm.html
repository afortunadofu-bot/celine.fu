從 0 建到 10+ 投資人 pipelineBuilt a 10+ investor pipeline from zero

成為所有募資動作的單一事實來源。A single source of truth for all fundraising.

背景Context

量化交易新創，技術做得出來，但對外一份材料都沒有，投資人會議是 0。A quant trading startup with working technology, zero outbound materials and zero investor meetings.

問題Problem

團隊講得出 Sharpe 跟回撤，但 LP 聽不懂；LP 想問的是「這跟我現在持有的部位比起來如何」。兩邊用的不是同一種語言。The team could talk Sharpe and drawdown; LPs could not. LPs wanted to know how it compared with what they already held. The two sides were not speaking the same language.

怎麼分析Analysis

先分清楚讀者層級：C-level 要三分鐘看完結論、投資經理要標準化的財務指標、技術盡職調查要模型細節。同一份東西寫三個版本會立刻不同步，所以改成一份文件分三層。Separate the readers: C-suite needs a three-minute conclusion, investment managers need standardised financial metrics, technical DD needs model detail. Three separate versions would drift apart immediately, so one document in three tiers.

解法Solution

15 頁白皮書加 Pitch Deck，把量化模型翻譯成 LP 讀得懂的財務敘事；GTM 拆成三條軌道分頭推進；投資人 CRM 記 3 個階段、5 類異議、轉換機率。A 15-page white paper and pitch deck translating the quant models into LP-readable financial narrative; GTM split into three parallel workstreams; an investor CRM tracking three stages, five objection categories and conversion probability.

結果Outcome

從零建立 10+ 投資人 pipeline，一份材料服務所有層級，募資動作有單一事實來源。A 10+ investor pipeline from zero, one document serving every reader level, and a single source of truth for fundraising.

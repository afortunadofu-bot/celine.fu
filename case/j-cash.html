背景Context

公司同時在跑 20 件以上的案子，每件都有自己的計價期程與下包付款條件。20+ concurrent projects, each with its own billing cycle and subcontractor payment terms.

問題Problem

沒有跨案的金流與排程視圖。進度只存在於每週的口頭會議，收款落後或相依卡住，往往等到開會才知道。No portfolio-wide view of cash or schedule. Status lived in a weekly verbal meeting, so late collections and blocked dependencies surfaced only at that meeting.

怎麼分析Analysis

先把「錢的狀態」拆成三段：已發包、已計價、已收款。延遲會卡在哪一段，決定要找的人完全不同——卡在計價是我們自己的事，卡在收款是業主的事，卡在發包是採購的事。把三段分開記，問題才有歸屬。I split cash into three states: committed, billed, collected. Where a delay sits determines who can unblock it — billing is ours, collection is the client's, commitment is sourcing's. Separating the three gave every delay an owner.

解法Solution

建互動式金流模型加上涵蓋全部專案的甘特主排程，兩者用同一組專案編號串起來，管理層與各專案看的是同一份。An interactive cash-flow model plus a Gantt master schedule covering every active project, joined on one project key so management and project teams read the same source.

結果Outcome

管理層第一次有單一視圖；團隊自己對照相依關係，不用等每週會議。內部期限固定壓在契約里程碑前一週。Management gained a single view; teams self-align on dependencies instead of waiting for the meeting. Internal deadlines now sit a week ahead of contractual milestones.

延伸Extension

同一套邏輯後來被我拿去做單一案子的請款預估與現金流分析——那份有完整的圖表與結論，已去識別化之後放在作品集裡。The same logic later became a full billing-forecast and cash-flow analysis for a single project — charted end to end, de-identified and published in the portfolio.

背景Context

一件智慧建築案裡的機電工程包，金額 4,000 萬元，是整個案子最大的一包。發包由我負責。The MEP package inside a smart-building project — NT$40M, the largest single package in the job. Sourcing was mine to run.

接手時的條件What I inherited

這一包是開工三個月之後換廠商，才找上我們。時程從接手那天就是趕的：送審、備料、人員報備這些前期行政作業全部來不及照正常節奏走，等於後面要用雙倍人力補做前面沒做完的事。議價不是在從容的狀態下談的，是一邊趕工一邊談。We were brought in three months after construction had started, replacing the previous vendor. The schedule was tight from day one: submissions, material lead times and personnel registration could not follow their normal rhythm, so later stages had to be double-staffed to catch up. The negotiation happened under that pressure, not in calm conditions.

問題Problem

20 家以上的下包各報各的，報價範圍互相重疊也互相漏接。同一項工作有三家報、另一項沒人報，總價看起來合理，但責任邊界是破的——真的照這樣發包，施工時每個介面都會吵。20+ subcontractors each quoted their own scope. The scopes overlapped in places and left gaps in others: three bids for the same work, none for the next item. The total looked reasonable, but the responsibility boundaries were broken — every interface would become a dispute on site.

怎麼分析Analysis

不比總價，先把每一份報價拆回「成本 × 範圍」兩個軸。做一張結構化評分表：每個工項逐一對照，誰有報、報多少、含不含輔料與測試、介面由誰負責。拆完之後才看得出來，價差大部分不是單價差異，是範圍認定不同。Instead of comparing totals, I decomposed every quote onto two axes: cost and scope. A structured scoring sheet listed each work item against every bid — who quoted it, at what price, whether consumables and testing were included, who owned the interface. Once decomposed, it was clear that most of the spread came from differing scope assumptions, not unit prices.

解法Solution

用那張表當議價的共同語言：把重疊的部分攤開讓廠商知道我清楚誰在報什麼，再把範圍重新切乾淨，整併成單一份執行契約。The sheet became the shared language for negotiation: overlaps were put on the table so vendors knew I could see exactly what each had quoted; scope was then re-cut cleanly and consolidated into one execution contract.

結果Outcome

較議價前基準降低 600 萬元（15%），而且範圍責任歸屬在簽約時就清楚，不留到施工期吵。NT$6M (15%) below the pre-negotiation baseline — and scope ownership was unambiguous at signing, not argued during construction.
